More About Me


Hey there! I'm Lokesh Goenka, an AI Engineer working across Speech AI, Conversational AI, and Generative AI.

Over the past year, I've worked on real-time conversational AI systems, speech technologies, LLM-powered applications, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). My current work involves building voice agents, evaluating STT/TTS and Audio-to-Audio models, and working with frameworks such as Google ADK, LiveKit, and AWS.

More recently, I've been working with TTS model fine-tuning and experimenting with speech representations, codecs, conditioning strategies, and model architectures to improve speech quality and domain-specific generation.

Before moving into Speech AI, my work focused on LLMs, RAG, and educational AI. During my Master's in Data Science and Computing at SSSIHL, I developed a Personalized Learning Assistant and worked on adaptive assessment and question generation using large language models.

I enjoy working at the intersection of research and engineering — understanding how models work, experimenting with them, and turning them into useful systems. Let us build things that matter.
